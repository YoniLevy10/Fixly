# Core Marketplace Business Rules

## Request Ownership

A request belongs to one customer.

---

## Professional Assignment

A request may have zero or one assigned professional.

---

## Status Rules

pending -> accepted -> in_progress -> completed

cancelled can happen before completion.

---

## Reviews

Reviews allowed only after completion.

---

## Verification

Professional verification is controlled internally.

Users cannot self-verify.

---

# MVP Constraints

Current MVP intentionally avoids:

- bidding systems
- auctions
- multi-provider negotiations
- dynamic pricing
- advanced dispatching
