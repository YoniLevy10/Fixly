# Professional Dashboard Flow

Purpose:

Operational control center for professionals.

---

# Core Sections

## Incoming Requests

Primary focus area.

Fast accept/decline.

## Active Jobs

Current in-progress work.

## Completed Jobs

History and reputation.

---

# UX Rules

- Operational clarity first
- Minimal distractions
- Large action buttons
- Status-first layout

---

# Critical Principle

Professionals are workers under pressure.

Do NOT design this like social media.

Design for speed.
