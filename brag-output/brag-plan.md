# Brag Plan: Fixly

## What is this app?
Fixly is a Hebrew-first home-repair marketplace that finds a tradesperson fast and tracks them live on a map — like Wolt, for fixing your house.

## The angle
Food delivery trained us to expect a live map for dinner. Fixly asks: why not for a plumber? The video sells that “Wolt for trades” instinct with real Hebrew UI — hero, categories, matching, and “בדרך.”

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Giant navy card, RTL: **זקוקים לתיקון?** — the exact home hero. Orange pulse on **בקשה מהירה**.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Category tiles pop in one by one: אינסטלציה · חשמל · מיזוג אוויר · נגרות
- Amber matching card: **מחפשים מקצוענים...** then **3 מקצוענים קיבלו את הבקשה**
- Live tracking strip: **איש המקצוע בדרך אליך** + “המיקום מתעדכן בזמן אמת (כמו Wolt)”

## Outro / punchline
Fixly logo (house + orange check) + **תיקונים חכמים**. Hold.

## User flow worth showing
Home hero → tap בקשה מהירה / pick category → matching wait → pro en route on the map.

## Tone
- Preset: `app-store`
- Creative direction: Wolt for home repairs — Hebrew marketplace launch
- Interpretation: Clean feature-card energy, RTL typography, snappy card reveals, no joke tone — the product is earnest.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#F2F4F7`
- Accent (secondary/CTA): `#F59E0B` / `#F97D10`
- Brand primary: `#123563`
- Text: `#0F1729`
- Display font: Heebo
- Body font: Heebo
- Strongest visual element: Navy rounded hero “זקוקים לתיקון?” with orange quick-request CTA

## Share copy (draft)
Fixly — תיקונים כמו Wolt. בקשה מהירה, מעקב חי, איש מקצוע בדרך.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm upbeat bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: volume ~0.32; soft fade-in 0–0.4s; fade under final logo last ~1.2s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset tempo ~120 BPM; strong cues near 16.02 / 17.02 / 18.02 for matching→tracking and outro; beat-grid for category tiles ~every other beat (~1s)
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; navy hero glow and orange CTA presence breathe with RMS/bass — no waveforms
- SFX posture: moderate app-store — light drops/clicks on cards, soft success on “בדרך”, logo hit on outro
- Audio-coupled moments: category tiles sequential; simulated tap on בקשה מהירה; matching card land; logo slam
- Restraint rule: no chaotic stacks; keep Hebrew copy readable over music

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook hero — 3.5s
Full-bleed soft gray. Navy rounded hero card (RTL) with **זקוקים לתיקון?** and subtitle **מצא את איש המקצוע המתאים / במהירות ובקלות**. Search pill placeholder **מה צריך לתקן?**. Worker emoji as in app. Orange **בקשה מהירה** button scales in; simulated tap ripple.
Sequential/interaction: yes — title hold, then CTA tap
Audio intent: confident open
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop on hero; click on tap
Music: upbeat bed starts
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Categories — 4.5s
Label **בחרו קטגוריה**. Four category cards appear one by one (RTL grid): 🔧 אינסטלציה · ⚡ חשמל · ❄️ מיזוג אוויר · 🪵 נגרות. Real card styling (white, rounded-2xl, accent chips).
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 cards beat-grid (~every other beat), then hold full set
Audio intent: light rhythmic arrivals
Audio-coupled idea: card-place / drop per tile
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Matching — 5.0s
Phone-ish / card frame recreating tracking wait UI: amber card **מחפשים מקצוענים...** then upgrades to **3 מקצוענים קיבלו את הבקשה — הראשון שמאשר יקבל**. Subtle pulse on spinner/dots.
Sequential/interaction: yes — status line swaps after ~2s
Audio intent: anticipation → confirmation
Audio-coupled idea: soft switch on status change
Transition mood: soft wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — En route — 4.0s
Tracking moment: map-like panel (simplified Fixly-blue pins) + copy **איש המקצוע בדרך אליך** and smaller **המיקום מתעדכן בזמן אמת (כמו Wolt)**. Status chip **בדרך**. Green trust line **אחריות Fixly** slides in near end.
Sequential/interaction: yes — map, then chip, then guarantee
Audio intent: payoff success
Audio-coupled idea: success ding when “בדרך” lands (near strong cue ~16–17s)
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3.0s
Centered Fixly icon (house + orange check on `#123563`) + wordmark **Fixly** + **תיקונים חכמים**. Hold.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: logo land, music fade
Audio-coupled idea: logo impact; music fades under
Transition mood: hold to black/end

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat app-store
**Audio summary:** Warm business bed under clean Hebrew UI reveals; light card/tap SFX; success hit into logo.
